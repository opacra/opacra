# Code of conduct

Opacra is built in the open, and everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, reviews,
the testnet and project channels) is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people reporting problems;
- criticise code and ideas, never people;
- keep discussion on the work, and in English unless a channel says otherwise;
- never ask anyone for their 25-word seed, keys or passwords, and never share someone's private
  details;
- not use project spaces to promote prices, trading, presales or any other coin;
- report security problems privately to dev@opacra.org rather than in public.

Harassment, insults, threats and discrimination of any kind are not accepted. Maintainers may
remove comments, close threads, or block anyone who breaks these rules, and will explain why
when they can.

To report a problem with someone's conduct, email dev@opacra.org. Reports are kept confidential.
